# Code of conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. Everyone participating in issues, pull requests, discussions and other
project spaces is expected to follow it.

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, welcome newcomers, and focus
criticism on ideas and code, not people. Harassment, personal attacks and publishing others'
private information are not tolerated.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers through a
[private report](https://github.com/Vlad-Movchan/specjig/security/advisories/new) (select
"code of conduct" in the description), or through GitHub's "Report content" option on the
comment or issue. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove content and
restrict participation as described in the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
